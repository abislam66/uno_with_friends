How to play
  • Match the top card by color, number or symbol. Wild cards go on anything.
  • No card to play? The game draws one for you. If it matches, you can play it.
  • Skip skips the next player. Reverse changes direction. +2 and +4 make the next player draw.
  • Stacking: if someone hits you with +2, play your own +2 to pass it on. The next player takes all 4!
  • Down to 2 cards? Hit UNO! before you play. Forget, and others can catch you for +2 cards.
  • Watch the timer. If it runs out, you draw a card and your turn ends.
  • First to empty their hand wins the round and scores points from everyone else's cards.
